# v1.18.2 — Conversation Evidence and Bounded Completion

**Release date:** 2026-09-09

**State:** Released as `v1.18.2`.

## Scope

- `/contract-refine` reviews relevant conversation evidence before proposing contract changes. It preserves original user attribution, project scope, chronology, durable-versus-task-only classification, and retrieval gaps. Historical instructions enter a proposal, not fresh authorization; unavailable retrieval permits only a clearly labelled docs-only audit.
- `/evidence-controller` derives a goal, scoped criteria, proof methods, and a compact conversation checkpoint. It continues authorized agent-owned work, checks actual consumer outcomes rather than treating green tests as sufficient, invalidates stale evidence, and closes with `blocked`, `needs_human`, or `agent_checked` as appropriate.
- A short `/evidence-controller <task>` suffices. With the controller and task context already established, `ทำต่อให้เสร็จ` requests continuation within existing authorization; the agent derives the goal and proof plan.
- These are skill-guidance changes, not a scheduler or host integration redesign. Existing approval and escalation boundaries remain in force.

## Limitations

- Automatic triggering, long-running tasks, compaction recovery, and host-level blocking of premature completion were not established by this trial.
- The checkpoint remains model-guided conversation state, not guaranteed durable state, a final-response interceptor, or an automatic wake/retry mechanism.
- Herdr reported done early while a child was still running. No Herdr or host integration fix is included; a child/status notification is not completion proof.
- `agent_checked` records agent-owned evidence, not human acceptance. Historical conversation retrieval does not confer renewed commit, push, deletion, or other action permission.
- The live completion trial does not establish exhaustive conversation retrieval or semantic correctness of contract-refinement decisions.
